Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FF Sensitivities (the “App”, “we”, “us”) handles information when you use the App. The App is published by ByteFlipper and does not require an account.

1. Information stored on your device

The App stores its settings, including language, theme, onboarding completion, and advertising frequency controls, on your device. This information is used to provide the App and is not uploaded to our own server.

2. Information processed by Google services

The Google Play version uses Google Firebase and Google Mobile Ads services. Depending on your device, settings, region, and consent choices, these services may process limited technical and app-use information, such as app version, operating-system version, device model, language, IP address, app-instance identifiers, crash diagnostics, and app interactions.

  • Firebase Analytics helps us understand aggregate App usage and improve the App.
  • Firebase Crashlytics receives crash and error diagnostics so we can fix stability issues.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging and In-App Messaging may use app-instance identifiers and, if notifications are allowed, a delivery token to send App messages.
  • Google AdMob serves and measures advertising, prevents fraud, and applies frequency limits.
  • Yandex Mobile Ads may serve an ad when selected through an AdMob mediation custom event.

Google handles this information under the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase Privacy and Security Information.

3. Advertising and your choices

The App may display banner, interstitial, and app-open advertisements provided by Google AdMob. The Yandex Advertising Network may also be selected through AdMob mediation for supported placements. Advertising services may process the Android Advertising ID, device and network information, approximate location derived from IP address, ad interactions, and diagnostic information. This is used to serve, measure, limit, and protect advertising from fraud. Ads may be personalised or non-personalised depending on your region, device settings, and consent choices.

Where required, the App uses Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) to request consent before using data for ad personalisation. You can review or change available choices at any time in Settings → Advertising & Privacy → Privacy Settings. You can also reset or delete your Android Advertising ID in your device's Google privacy or ads settings.

More information is available in Google's Advertising Technologies Policy and the Yandex Privacy Policy.

4. Information you choose to send

If you contact support, submit a bug report, or request sensitivity settings, we receive the information you include in that request. Please do not include sensitive personal information unless it is necessary to resolve your request.

5. Permissions

  • Internet and network state are used to load App data, Google services, and ads.
  • Advertising ID is used by Google Mobile Ads where permitted by your settings and consent.
  • Post notifications is optional and is requested by Android; you can refuse or revoke it in system settings.

6. Data retention and deletion

Data stored by the App remains on your device until you clear the App's storage or uninstall it. Google and Firebase retain data they process according to their own retention policies. You can manage or delete local App data in Android settings, change advertising choices in the App or device settings, and contact Google for requests relating to data processed by Google services.

7. Children's privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the App or its services change. The current version is available in the App; the date at the top identifies the latest revision.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact ByteFlipper at byteflipper.business@gmail.com.
